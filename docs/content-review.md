# Content revision and release review

The October 2026 revision preserves 30 stable pack IDs while replacing vague
or novelty-led collections, adding 300 authored clues, and separating word
entries from final connection explanations. Changed titles retain their old
titles for existing device exclusion lists.

The first clue gives context; the second adds an association. A second pass
softened definition-like clues for RECTO, VERSO, UMBRA, MOLAR, SPINE, YEAST,
VIGIL, SINEW, CHARD, QUOTA, LAGER, LYMPH, and FOLIO. Ratings are editorial,
not measured difficulty estimates. Easier packs provide variation between
more demanding literary discoveries; uniform obscurity is not the objective.

## Validation status and human release gate

Structural validation checks length, dictionary membership, duplicates,
required content, exact answer leakage, obvious letter-position giveaways,
and duplicate clues. Go checks embedded content at startup as well. These
checks do not prove a theme is interesting or a clue is fair.

Automated browser pilot runs cover Public Influence, Shades of Feeling,
An Open Book, Fall and Rise, and The Heist. The tests know the answers and
validate the interface and progression; **they are not blind human playtests**.
No player difficulty or satisfaction results are claimed.

Before publishing the revised pool, have people who have not seen the
answers play those five packs first. Record:

- Can they explain word progress and the hidden connection without prompting?
- After each word, what do they think the connection might be?
- Which unknown words felt discoverable, and which felt arbitrary?
- Did a hint help them choose a next guess, or simply announce the answer?
- Does each final explanation feel specific and fair?
- Can they leave, return, and revise their private theory comfortably?

Revise the pilots from that feedback before expanding the player review to
the other packs. Do not treat an automated pass or an agent editorial pass as
human approval. Check remaining historical notes against reliable references;
prefer a verified usage note whenever an origin cannot be substantiated.
