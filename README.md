<h1 align="center">Wordle</h1>

<p align="center"><em>Solve words. Keep the good ones.</em></p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://wordle.jerkeyray.com"><strong>Play →</strong></a>
</p>

<br />

A word game for curious people. Six guesses, one word — and every word you
solve is yours to keep.

**Classic** — one word, on its own.\
**Themed** — five words, one hidden connection.\
**Together** — a daily board shared with a friend, taking turns.

Every solved word lands in your own little dictionary, with its meaning and a
note on how it's used.
